🏛️ Governance Hierarchy

Role + Jurisdiction Scoped Access (RBAC + ABAC):

  • Central Admin: Nationwide visibility, SLA policy configuration & audit logs.
  • State Admin: State-scoped (e.g. Andhra Pradesh), district escalation reviews.
  • District Admin: District-scoped (e.g. Guntur), inspector team assignment & issues.
⏳ SLA Duration Policies
  • CRITICAL (4h SLA) CCTV Blackout / DVR Tampering
  • HIGH (24h SLA) Geofence Offset / Biometric Anomaly
  • MEDIUM (72h SLA) Health Score Drop Below 60%
  • LOW (7d SLA) Routine Audit Follow-Up
🔄 Automated SLA Breach Escalation Flow

When an issue is logged at District level (L1), the SLA clock starts. If unhandled within the specified SLA duration, the Automated Escalation Engine automatically promotes the issue to State Admin (L2) and subsequently to Central Command (L3) with internal audit thread tracking.

⚖️ Section 63B Admissibility Certificate

Under Bharatiya Sakshya Adhiniyam (BSA) 2023, electronic evidence submitted to courts or administrative tribunals requires a signed Section 63B Certificate.

  • SHA-256 Hash Lock: Cryptographic hash computed at capture time prevents post-inspection tampering.
  • IST & GPS Telemetry: Indian Standard Time (+05:30) and hardware reverse-geocoded location (*Brodipet, Guntur, AP ±4m*).
  • NIC Device Signature: Sealed with authorized officer digital signature.
